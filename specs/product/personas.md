# Personas and roles

## Personas
### P1: Brand owner (Adrián)
- Context: runs the brand and its Instagram account; owns the Meta Business, Supabase and Fly.io accounts.
- Goals: publish good content often without spending hours editing; spend on ads only for content that works.
- Frustrations: manual editing, guessing what will perform, losing track of what was published.

### P2: Editor
- Context: helps produce and schedule content.
- Goals: generate, review and schedule quickly within the brand's voice.

## Roles and permissions
| Role | Who | Can | Cannot |
|---|---|---|---|
| owner | Brand owner | Everything, including Settings (providers, spending limit, templates), ads and user management | |
| editor | Team members | Libraries, generation, review, discovery, scheduling without ads | Settings, ads, users |
